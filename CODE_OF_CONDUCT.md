# Code of conduct

This repository exists to help students find real opportunities. Everyone who opens an issue, comments or sends a pull
request is expected to keep it a useful, respectful place.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with first-time contributors and students asking questions.
- Stick to facts you can link to: an official page, a posting, a published notice.
- Disagree with listings, not with people.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or comments about someone's caste, religion, gender, region, language or background.
- Posting anyone's e-mail address, phone number, home address or profile link, including your own.
- Naming or accusing a private individual. Caution reports are about schemes, domains and organisations, and need
  links or screenshots that back them up.
- Promotion: paid listings, agency or consultancy adverts, referral codes, or "DM me" offers.
- Spam, or opening many issues for the same thing.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or hide comments, close issues and pull requests, and block accounts that break these rules.
Serious or repeated cases are reported to GitHub.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Don't post personal details in
a public issue.
